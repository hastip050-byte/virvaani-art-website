## Public / Bridge Art reference

Bridge/public-space art direction was researched from Geo India Foundation's Public Space Beautification programme: https://www.geoindiafoundation.org/programs/beautification.php

Note: the site describes bridge murals, subway art, infrastructure pillars and park/community walls. The exact third-party photograph is not bundled in this ZIP unless licensed for redistribution; the VIRVAANI site therefore does not falsely present it as a VIRVAANI project.
